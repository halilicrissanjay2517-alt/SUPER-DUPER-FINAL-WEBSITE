Our Patron & Heritage

Guided by Saint Patrick

Saint Patrick's Academy, Inc. is named for the missionary bishop who brought the Christian faith to Ireland. Captured as a young man and held in slavery, he escaped and later returned to the land of his captivity to serve the people who had enslaved him — a life that stands, above all, for perseverance, service, and forgiveness.

Our history

Founded in 1968 as a non-stock, non-profit Catholic institution, Saint Patrick's Academy has served the families of Dingalan and the wider province of Aurora for more than half a century. What began as a small parish school grew, class by class, into the academy it is today — formed by the parishes, teachers, and parents who gave it their time.

Under the patronage of Saint Patrick, the school carries forward a simple mission: to form students who are competent in their studies, grounded in their faith, and generous in service to their community.
