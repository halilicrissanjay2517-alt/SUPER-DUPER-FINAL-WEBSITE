Where we began

Our history

From a small parish school to a full secondary academy.

A parish school grows up

Founded in 1968 as a non-stock, non-profit Catholic institution, Saint Patrick's Academy began as a small parish school with a handful of classrooms and a simple purpose: to give the children of Dingalan a solid Catholic education close to home. What started with a few classes grew, year by year, into the academy it is today.

For more than half a century the school has been shaped by the parishes, teachers, and parents who gave it their time — generations of families who sent their children, and then their grandchildren, through the same gates. That continuity is the school's real history.

Where we stand today

Today the academy offers complete secondary education from Grade 7 to Grade 12, with academic strands, a vibrant campus ministry, and one of the province's most recognised marching bands. We remain non-stock and non-profit: every peso that comes in goes back into teaching, facilities, and the students themselves.

Our patron & heritage

Guided by Saint Patrick

The saint whose life gave the school its name and its values.

Who he was

Saint Patrick's Academy, Inc. is named for the missionary bishop who brought the Christian faith to Ireland. Captured as a young man and held in slavery, he escaped and later returned to the land of his captivity to serve the people who had enslaved him — a life that stands, above all, for perseverance, service, and forgiveness.

What his example asks of us

Under the patronage of Saint Patrick, the school carries forward a simple mission: to form students who are competent in their studies, grounded in their faith, and generous in service to their community. His story is the school's own shorthand for resilience — the idea that hardship can be turned into service.

Our mission: to form young men and women who are competent in their studies, grounded in their faith, and generous in service to their community.

Milestones

Our timeline

The moments that marked the school's growth.

  1. 1968

    The school is founded

    Saint Patrick's Academy opens as a small non-stock, non-profit parish school in Dingalan, Aurora, with its first classes and its first teachers.

  2. 1970s

    Steady growth

    Enrolment rises with the town, classrooms are added, and the school settles into its role as the community's Catholic secondary school.

  3. 1980s

    The marching band is formed

    The school band is organised and quickly becomes a fixture of town fiestas and provincial parades — the beginning of a long winning tradition.

  4. 1990s

    First major titles

    The band and academic teams begin bringing home provincial honours, and the academy's name travels well beyond Dingalan.

  5. 2000s

    Expansion and facilities

    New classrooms, a covered court, and a dedicated band hall are built as the school grows to serve more families across Aurora.

  6. 2010s

    Senior high school

    The academy completes its Grade 7–12 offering, opening academic strands so students can finish their secondary years on one campus.

  7. Today

    One school, one portal

    With this Student Portal, announcements, enrollment, records, and payments now live in one place for students and parents.
