Edit student

Update any field and save. Changes are written to the Excel database.

Tuition

The fee is Tuition Fee + Miscellaneous Fee, and the balance is Total Fee − Total Paid, so it always matches the payment history. Record payments in Record payment; they cannot be typed in here.

Record a payment

The amount is deducted from the student's balance automatically, and today's date is saved as their last payment date.

Import from Excel

Choose an .xlsx file with a Students sheet (a Student ID column is required; name, grade, section, fees, guardian and contact are read when present) and optionally a Payments sheet (student ID, amount, date, receipt). Existing students are updated, new ones are added, and payments are matched by receipt number so re-importing never double-counts. Balances are recalculated after.
