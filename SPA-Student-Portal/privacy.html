Privacy & cookies

What the student portal stores, why it stores it, and the measures that keep it safe. Written plainly, so a student or a parent can read it in full.

01
Cookies

One cookie, and only when you sign in

No advertising cookies. No analytics cookies. No third-party cookies.

What is set

When you sign in, the server sets a single session cookie — spa_student_sid for a student account, spa_admin_sid for an office account. It holds a random identifier that tells the server who you are for the rest of the visit.

How it is protected

  • HttpOnly — scripts on the page cannot read it, so a bad link or an injected script cannot copy your session.
  • Secure — sent only over HTTPS once the site is served that way.
  • SameSite=Lax — another website cannot make a browser send it with a forged request.
  • Expires after 8 hours — and is deleted the moment you sign out.

Strictly necessary. This cookie is what keeps you signed in; the portal cannot work without it. That is why the notice on each page does not turn analytics on or off — there is nothing optional to turn on.

Small preferences

One small value lives in your browser's own storage, not in a cookie: whether you have acknowledged this notice, so it does not reappear on every page. It stays on your device and is never sent to the school. Anything you type into a search box is sent with that one look-up and is not stored by your browser.

02
Records

What the school holds about you

Only what the registrar's office needs to run enrollment and tuition.

Student record

Student ID, name, grade and section, enrollment status, guardian and contact details, and the tuition figures — total fee, amount paid, remaining balance and payment history.

Account

The email you sign up with and a salted hash of your password. Your password itself is never stored or shown back to anyone. The office may also record whether an account was approved, by whom, and when.

Activity log

Who signed in and which record was changed — kept so a mistake or a disputed payment can be traced.

All of this lives in one school-owned spreadsheet on the school's own server. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or sent to any outside service.

What is public

  • School announcements
  • Grade and section for a Student ID you know
  • A count of enrolled students
  • Never fees or balances
03
Protection

How your account is protected

Defences that apply to every request, not just the ones that look suspicious.

  • Sessions are kept in a cookie no script can read, and end when you sign out.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Repeated wrong passwords are throttled, per address and per route, to stop guessing.
  • Every response forbids framing, type sniffing and plugin content, and states exactly which scripts the page may run.
  • Requests that did not come from this site are refused.
  • Fees and balances are returned only to the signed-in student they belong to.
  • The office can withdraw an account at any moment, which ends its open sessions immediately.

If something looks wrong

Sign out, then contact the registrar's office at (042) 555-0168 so the account can be reviewed.

04
Your choices

Seeing, correcting, and leaving

Your record is yours to check.

Sign in at any time to see your own record and payment history, and to change your password. To correct a name, grade, section or contact detail, ask the registrar's office — school records are changed there so the office's copy stays the authoritative one.

To have your account closed, ask the office. Your student record remains part of the school's academic history, but the login is removed and its sessions end at once.
