01
Service one

Announcements & reminders

School updates land here first, so you never miss a deadline.

What it is for

Every notice the administration posts — class suspensions, exam schedules, enrollment windows, and office advisories — appears in one dated feed. Categories are colour-tagged so you can scan for the ones that concern you.

How to use it

  1. Open your account and go to the announcements feed.
  2. Filter by category — enrollment, payment, records, or general.
  3. Read the dated entry, then follow any linked section for details.

Tip: the latest three updates are always mirrored on the home page panel, so a quick check there is often enough.

02
Service two

Enrollment

Requirements, steps, and deadlines for every grade level.

What it is for

A walk-through of the enrollment process from form request to section assignment, with the exact documents the registrar needs and the dates that matter.

How to use it

  1. Check the current enrollment window and deadline.
  2. Prepare the listed requirements before visiting the office.
  3. Submit to the registrar and keep your reference number.

Requirements

  • Report card / Form 138
  • Good moral certificate
  • Birth certificate (PSA copy)
  • 2×2 ID photographs
03
Service three

Student records

Your details, grade level, section, and account status.

What it is for

A read-only view of the record the school holds for you — name, grade level, section, standing, and any balance on your account. If something looks wrong, the records office corrects it at source.

How to use it

  1. Sign in with your student account.
  2. Open the records section and check each field.
  3. Report any discrepancy to the records office with your ID.

Note: records shown in the portal are a convenience copy. The signed documents on file at the office remain the official reference.

What is shown

  • Full name and student ID
  • Grade level and section
  • Enrollment status
  • Outstanding balance
04
Service four

Payments & balances

How to pay, and how to read your own payment history.

What it is for

Each payment you make is recorded against your account with a receipt number, so your balance always reflects what has actually been settled — and you keep a full history rather than a bare total.

How to use it

  1. Check your current balance before paying.
  2. Pay through the cashier or an accepted channel.
  3. Keep the receipt number — it is the reference for any query.

Accepted methods

  • Cash (cashier)
  • Bank deposit
  • Online transfer
  • GCash
  • Check

Ready to sign in?

Use the account issued by the registrar's office.
